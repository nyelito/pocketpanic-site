About the game

Your phone calls out a move — shake it, flip it, tilt it, blow on it, cover it, swipe it — and you do it. Then it asks for the next one, a little faster. Pocket Panic reads the phone's motion sensors, microphone, proximity sensor and volume buttons, so you're moving the phone around rather than tapping buttons. Every command is spoken out loud, so you can play without looking at the screen.

The headline mode is Pass the Panic: 2 to 8 people share one phone. Do your moves, pass it on, and hope you're not holding it when the hidden fuse runs out. It's built for the moment everyone at the table is shouting at whoever is holding the phone.

Features

  • Pass the Panic: hot-potato party mode for 2–8 players on one phone, with a hidden fuse and lives.
  • Classic: one mistake and it's over.
  • Power Panic: golden moves give power-ups (Freeze, Shield, Double points), streaks multiply your score, and chaos rounds twist the rules.
  • 3 Lives and Blitz (60 seconds), plus an untimed Practice mode.
  • Leaderboards for each solo mode and 17 achievements on Google Play Games.
  • No ads, no subscriptions, no coins or energy. One purchase unlocks everything.
  • Private by design: the microphone only measures how hard you blow. Nothing is recorded.
