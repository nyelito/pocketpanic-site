Pocket Panic Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

Short version: Pocket Panic has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't collect or sell your personal information. The microphone is only used to hear you blow on the phone during a game. Nothing is ever recorded or sent anywhere.

Microphone

One of the moves in the game is "Blow it". To detect it, Pocket Panic listens to the microphone while a game is being played and checks how loud the sound is, right on your phone. Audio is never recorded, saved, or sent off your device. The microphone is off on the menus and whenever the app is in the background. You can say no to the microphone permission and still play; the game just won't ask you to blow.

Motion sensors and touch

The game reads your phone's motion sensors (to detect shaking, flipping and tilting), the proximity sensor (to detect covering the screen), the volume buttons while a game is running, and your taps and swipes. These readings are only used during play, on your phone, and are never stored or sent anywhere.

What stays on your phone

The app saves a few things on your device so the game remembers them: your settings, your best scores, the player names you type in for Pass the Panic, how many free games you have left, and whether you've unlocked the full game. This information isn't sent to us. If you use Android's backup, Android may include it in your own Google backup so it comes back when you restore a phone.

Google Play Games

If you're signed in to Google Play Games, the game uses it for leaderboards. Google then handles your Play Games profile (such as your gamer name and player ID) and the scores you submit, under the Google Privacy Policy. We can see leaderboard scores and the public gamer names attached to them, like any other player.

Purchases

Pocket Panic can be unlocked with a one-time purchase through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment; we never see your payment details. The app receives a purchase confirmation from Google Play so it can unlock the game and check that the purchase is genuine.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the app opens Android's share sheet with a short message about your score. You choose where it goes; nothing is shared unless you send it.

Children

Pocket Panic is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

What we don't do

We don't show ads, use analytics or tracking tools, build profiles, or sell or share personal information with anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
